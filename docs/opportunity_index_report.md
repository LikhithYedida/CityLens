# CityLens Opportunity Index

**379 of 393 U.S. metropolitan areas scored.** Housing as of Sep 2026; jobs and wages as of Mar 2026; migration 2021-2023; permits 2023-2025.

**Question:** where does a typical paycheck go furthest today, in a place that is still growing?

## How it's scored

| Pillar | Weight | Indicators |
|---|---|---|
| Affordability | 40% | Rent / income, Mortgage / income |
| Momentum | 35% | Job growth (3-yr, yearly), Paycheck growth minus price growth |
| Demand & Supply | 25% | Net migration per 1,000, New homes permitted per 1,000 |

Each indicator is converted to a 0-100 percentile rank (100 = best), pillars average their indicators, and the index is the weighted sum. Metros missing an entire pillar are not ranked.

## Does the ranking hold up?

**Random weights.** Every metro was re-ranked under 1,000 random pillar weightings, drawn evenly from every possible mix. **6 metros stay in the top 25 in at least 80% of them**, so they rank highly no matter how you weight the pillars:

- Huntsville, AL: top 25 in 99% of weightings (median rank 3)
- Austin-Round Rock-San Marcos, TX: top 25 in 96% of weightings (median rank 3)
- Sherman-Denison, TX: top 25 in 94% of weightings (median rank 2)
- Raleigh-Cary, NC: top 25 in 95% of weightings (median rank 4)
- Fayetteville-Springdale-Rogers, AR: top 25 in 86% of weightings (median rank 7)
- Greeley, CO: top 25 in 85% of weightings (median rank 12)

**Drop one indicator.** Re-scoring without each indicator in turn:

| Indicator removed | Rank correlation with baseline | Top-25 overlap |
|---|---|---|
| Rent / income | 0.89 | 68% |
| Mortgage / income | 0.93 | 76% |
| Job growth (3-yr, yearly) | 0.88 | 68% |
| Paycheck growth minus price growth | 0.88 | 68% |
| Net migration per 1,000 | 0.96 | 80% |
| New homes permitted per 1,000 | 0.96 | 92% |

A rank correlation near 1.0 means removing that input barely changes the ordering.

## Caveats

- Rent and mortgage ratios use the area's median household income, not any one person's.
- Mortgage payment is principal and interest only (20% down, 30-year fixed); taxes and insurance are excluded.
- Migration data ends in 2023 (the IRS publishes about two years late), and the latest year uses the IRS's new matching method.
- Ranks within a few places of each other are effectively ties; see the 90% rank range.
